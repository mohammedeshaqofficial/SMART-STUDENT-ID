New resident

Card registration

Link a resident to their NFC card. A User ID such as USR004 is generated automatically, and their digital profile goes live at once.

Colons and spaces are fine: 04:a7:b2:91:83

How it works

Three quiet steps

  1. Read the card's UID on the ESP32 Serial Monitor, the scanner page, or with your phone.
  2. Fill in the resident's details and register.
  3. The card opens the door right away, and the profile is shareable by QR.

Only the UID is linked to the card. Personal details stay in the database.
