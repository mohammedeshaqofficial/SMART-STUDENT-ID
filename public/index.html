What residents get

Built for a building that knows who belongs.

Tap-to-enter access

Hold the card to the RC522 reader. The ESP32 asks the backend, then the servo unlocks or stays shut.

Web scanner

Verify cards from any phone: tap with Web NFC on Android, scan a profile QR code, or type a UID at the front desk.

Digital identity

A shareable resident profile with a QR code, linked to the card.

Nothing on the card

Student data lives in the database. The card holds only its UID.

Real-time checks

Every tap is verified live, so a blocked card stops working at once.

Complete access history

Granted and denied attempts are logged with time, room, reason and source, then searchable and exportable.

Warden control

Block a lost card in one click, re-activate it, or issue a replacement that retires the old one.

“The card stores only its unique UID. The same UID links to a User ID and the resident's digital profile, connecting hostel access with digital identity.”
